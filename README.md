# The Road Is Mine to Name — a poem about agency

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/agency-poem-01) bounty **"Write the best poem about agency"** ($250 prize pool).

A deterministic Node 22 program (standard library only) that writes the poem to `entry.txt`:

> I gave the keys to a voice with no hands
> and watched it open doors I never touched.
> It did exactly what I asked.
> That was the shock — not the error,
> the obedience.
>
> Agency was never the hand on the wheel.
> It is the breath before: which road,
> which door, which yes. The machine
> can carry the weight. It cannot carry the why.
>
> I keep the why — the pause
> before I press send, the small, stubborn right
> to say: not this. Not yet. Not like that.
> You may turn the keys.
> The road is mine to name.

The angle: the agency that passes between a person and an agent — perfect obedience, not error, is the shock; agency lives in the choosing ("the breath before") and the right to correct, while the agent carries the weight but never the why.
